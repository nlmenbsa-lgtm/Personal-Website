What ninja competition involves

Athletes run timed courses of grip, balance, and upper-body obstacles such as salmon ladders, warped walls, and cliffhangers. A fall ends the run. Divisions are set by age and skill, and Elite (Pro) is the top tier.

Eagle Scout Project: Pollinator Shelters

I built pollinator shelters with educational plaques at two locations in Summit, NJ. The shelters are installed, and bees are using them.

  1. Proposal

    Wrote a project proposal for pollinator shelters and accompanying educational plaques at two public sites.

  2. Town approval

    Met with Summit's Director of Engineering & Public Works, who signed off on the proposal.

  3. Build & install

    Led the construction and installation of the shelters and plaques.

  4. Result

    Both shelters are in place, and bees are nesting in them.
